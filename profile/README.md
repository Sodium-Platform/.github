# Sodium Platform

**Building useful software. Exploring technology. Creating a better digital future.**

Sodium Platform is a student-led technology initiative focused on software development, cybersecurity, open-source innovation, and practical technology solutions.

We believe great technology starts with curiosity, collaboration, and the willingness to build. Our mission is to learn, experiment, and develop useful tools while growing a community of responsible technology creators.

## What We Do

- **Software Development** — Building desktop applications, utilities, and developer tools.
- **Cybersecurity** — Exploring defensive security, privacy, and responsible security research.
- **Open Source** — Sharing projects, learning together, and welcoming community contributions.
- **Research & Innovation** — Exploring emerging technologies and turning ideas into practical projects.

## Our First Product

### Sodium File Manager

Our initial product is Sodium File Manager, a Windows file management application.

We are working toward a reliable, useful, and user-friendly experience, improving the product through development, testing, and feedback.

*Project links and release information will be added when publicly available.*

## Our Principles

- **Build with purpose:** Solve real problems with useful technology.
- **Security matters:** Treat privacy, safety, and responsible practices as essential.
- **Learn continuously:** Improve our skills through experimentation and collaboration.
- **Work openly:** Share knowledge and welcome constructive feedback.
- **Respect contributors:** Value teamwork, transparency, and appropriate credit.

## Join the Journey

We are at the beginning of our journey, and there is plenty to build.

Explore our public repositories, share feedback, report issues, and contribute to projects when contribution guidelines are available.

## Our Vision

To grow Sodium Platform into a technology organization that creates useful software, supports open-source innovation, advances responsible cybersecurity, and serves users around the world.
